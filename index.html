What the benchmarks look like

Benchmarks are tied to broker eligibility and your development, not sales quotas.

  • Two years of active licensed experience
  • Transactions logged toward the state's experience requirement
  • Broker qualifying coursework completed
  • Regular training and meeting attendance
  • Performance targets set together at the start

Passing the broker exam is up to you. We make sure you're prepared and supported, but no license outcome is guaranteed.

This is for you if
